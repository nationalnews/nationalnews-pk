Privacy Policy

This notice explains the current privacy position of the NationalNews.pk preview website.

Current status: The preview homepage does not submit or store newsletter sign-up details. This policy must be updated before analytics, accounts, comments, newsletters or other data-collection features are activated.

Information collected

We do not currently offer visitor accounts or an active newsletter signup on this preview site. Our hosting provider may process technical request data as part of delivering and protecting the website; refer to the provider's applicable privacy information for details.

Cookies and analytics

We have not intentionally added third-party advertising trackers or analytics to this preview. If such tools are introduced, this policy should be revised to explain their use.

Children and sensitive information

Please do not send sensitive personal information through ordinary email. We aim to avoid publishing private information without a clear and proportionate public-interest reason.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated as the website's features evolve. Questions can be sent to editor@nationalnews.pk.
