About NationalNews.pk

Pakistan Ki Awaz — a developing digital publication focused on clear, responsible and useful news for readers in Pakistan and beyond.

Launch status: This website is currently in preview. Live newsroom operations and regular news publishing are still being developed.

Our mission

We aim to make important public affairs easier to understand through accurate reporting, clear sourcing and helpful context. Our goal is to distinguish verified facts from opinion, analysis and developing information.

Our standards

We intend to attribute sources, correct material errors transparently, respect privacy and require editorial review before publication. Read our Editorial Policy for more detail.

For feedback, corrections or general enquiries, email editor@nationalnews.pk.
